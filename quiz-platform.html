Project overview

The platform uses RAG and embeddings to turn textbook content into MCQs. Administrators manage teachers and assign subjects, teachers create and conduct online tests, and students receive automatically graded results sent to their teachers.

From textbooks to questions

The system uses embeddings to retrieve relevant textbook material and generates multiple-choice questions from that context.

Teacher and administrator workflows

Administrators manage teachers and assign subjects. Teachers use the quiz material to create and conduct online tests.

Student results

Students complete tests online. The system grades their submissions automatically and sends the results to their teachers.
