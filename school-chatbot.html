Project overview

Guardrails help keep responses appropriate for a school setting. The Study Buddy explains topics in a more detailed, reading-oriented style, while the General Assistant is designed to respond briefly and accurately.

Study Buddy

This mode provides detailed, reading-oriented explanations so students can follow a topic step by step.

General Assistant

This mode uses concise responses when a short explanation or direct answer is more suitable.

School context

Guardrails help keep responses appropriate for a school setting while students use either response style.
